\ArticleAbstract{We propose SAGE-CREH, a fifth architecture combining skill selection, a direct path, independent solvers and conditional adjudication. A focused review of twelve arXiv papers informs the design. After 20 separate development runs, 216 live runs used the same model and twelve original tasks, two repetitions and nine conditions: four architectures, a single agent, SAGE and three ablation controls. Replication consumed 707,090 tokens in 510 calls. SAGE achieved 23/24 strict successes and 23/24 after normalizing only the SQL envelope, averaging 1,765 tokens and 7.34 seconds per task. Explicit controls distinguish coordination, instruction loading and output format. The sample is small, synthetic and known during design; this is a development-informed replication, not evidence of universal superiority or blind generalization.}
