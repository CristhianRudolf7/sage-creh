\ArticleAbstract{We review nine arXiv papers first submitted in 2026 and execute a small comparison of sequential, parallel, hierarchical and reflexive subagent management, with a single-agent baseline and a skill-loading ablation. Twelve synthetic tasks, two repetitions and six conditions produce 144 live runs and 416 model calls using GPT-5.6 Luna with low reasoning effort. The single agent completes 20/24 strict tasks with 1\,143 tokens and 4.72 seconds per run on average; the four team configurations complete 19--20/24. A post-hoc SQL-wrapper sensitivity changes total completions from 120/144 to 142/144 without changing query text. Loading all skills increases hierarchical mean tokens by 94.8\%. These exploratory results quantify overhead on short, fully supplied tasks and do not establish a universal best architecture or long-running autonomy.}
